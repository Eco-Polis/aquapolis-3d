# Aquopolis: The Undersea City of Eco-Polis

Interactive 3D model of **Aquopolis**, an undersea city built for an English project.

**View it in 3D:** https://ledat0505.github.io/aquapolis-3d/ (drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap a building to see its purpose).

`aquapolis.json` lists the 1,078 objects of the city (model, position, rotation, scale and description): the glass dome, residential towers, the Aquopolis Core, the elevated maglev and undersea metro, the kelp farms and coral reef, and the floating port at the surface.

**Team:** Bảo Ngọc, Phương Lan, Khánh Uyên, Quỳnh Anh, Lam Giang

3D models: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) game asset kits (CC0 license).
